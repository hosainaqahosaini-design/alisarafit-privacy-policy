# Privacy Policy for AlisaraFit

**Last Updated:** July 1, 2026

AlisaraFit is a health and fitness application developed by **Hosain aqa Hosaini**.

## Information We Collect

AlisaraFit may store the following information that you voluntarily enter:

- Age
- Gender
- Height
- Weight
- Activity Level
- Daily Water Intake
- Weight Goal
- Progress Records
- BMI History
- Nutrition Preferences

## How We Use Information

The information you enter is used only to provide:

- BMI calculations
- Daily calorie calculations
- Nutrition planning
- Exercise recommendations
- Water tracking
- Progress tracking
- Achievement statistics

## Local Storage

In the current version, all user information is stored locally on your own device using SharedPreferences.

No personal health information is uploaded to our servers.

## Advertising

Future versions of AlisaraFit may include Google AdMob advertisements.

Google may collect anonymous advertising identifiers according to its own Privacy Policy.

## Analytics

The current version does not use Firebase Analytics or any third-party analytics service.

## Medical Disclaimer

AlisaraFit is intended for educational and informational purposes only.

The application does not provide medical diagnosis or treatment.

Always consult your physician before making important health or fitness decisions.

## Children's Privacy

AlisaraFit is not intended for children under the age of 13.

## Data Security

Reasonable security measures are used to protect user information stored on the device.

However, no electronic storage method is completely secure.

## Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated at any time.

Changes will be published on this page.
